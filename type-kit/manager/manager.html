Welcome to Type Kit. Type a shortcut such as /sig or ;addr in any text field and it expands. Backspace right after an expansion undoes it. Press Alt+Shift+V on any page to search snippets and your clipboard history. Starter snippets are below; edit them or make your own.

          Export
