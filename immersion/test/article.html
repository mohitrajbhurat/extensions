A Weekend in the Mountains

Last summer my family decided to leave the city for a few days. We packed the car with food, water and warm clothes, and drove north toward the mountains. My father was happy because he loves the forest, and my mother wanted to see the lake near the old village.

The road was long and narrow. After three hours we stopped at a small restaurant where the waiter brought us bread, cheese and coffee. My sister ordered a chocolate cake, and my brother drank a glass of orange juice. It was a beautiful morning and the sky was clear.

When we arrived at the hotel, the weather changed. Dark clouds covered the sun and the wind became cold. The rain fell all night, but the room was warm and the bed was soft. I read a book while my parents talked about the history of the region.

The next day we walked to the river. The water was very cold, but the children wanted to swim anyway. We saw a bird, two horses and a dog that followed us for an hour. My grandmother told an old story about a king and a queen who lived in a castle on the hill.

In the afternoon we visited a museum and a church. The museum had paintings by a famous artist and a library with ancient books. The teacher who worked there explained the meaning of every picture. It was a strange and wonderful experience.

On Sunday we returned home. The journey was quiet; everyone was tired but happy. I learned that a simple trip can teach you more than a week at school. Next year we want to visit the beach and the island in the south. Thank you for reading, and good luck with your own adventure!

Practical notes

If you plan a similar trip, check the weather, bring a map and keep some money in your pocket. The station is close to the airport, and the bus to the village leaves every day at eight in the morning.
