Language Immersion

You're set up

From now on a handful of words on the pages you read are quietly swapped into your target language. Hover one to see the original, hear it pronounced, and tell the extension whether you already know it.

  • Pick your target language and how many words per page below.
  • Open the toolbar icon to pause immersion on a site.
  • Words you meet build a review deck with spaced repetition and quizzes.
