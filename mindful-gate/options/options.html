Welcome to Mindful Gate

Plain blockers get switched off. Mindful Gate asks for a moment instead: when you open a distracting site, you name your intention, take a short breath, and get a time-boxed pass. When time is up, you decide again.

  1. Review the starter list below and add the sites that pull you in.
  2. Set a hard-block schedule for the hours you protect (optional, with strict mode).
  3. Start a focus session from the toolbar button. Distractions are fully blocked while it runs.

Sites and schedules

Every site in a group is a distraction. Subdomains are included (youtube.com covers m.youtube.com).

Mindful gate

Focus timer
