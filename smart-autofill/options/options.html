How it works

  1. Fill in a profile. Add work history, education, documents and answers to common application questions.
  2. Open a job application or any long form, click the toolbar button, pick a profile and press Preview & fill.
  3. Matched fields are outlined: green is high confidence, amber medium, red low. Click the check mark on a badge to skip a field, or use its menu to map it to a different profile field. Mappings you choose are remembered for that site.
  4. Press Fill on the in-page panel. Use Undo fill to restore the previous values.
  5. Correct anything that is wrong. If you change a field to another value from your profile, Smart Autofill learns that mapping for the site.

Answer templates

Custom answers can include {company}, {role}, {name}, {current_company}, {current_title}, {years} and {date}. Company and role are guessed from the page (job board URL, page title, heading).

Files

Resumes and cover letters are stored inside the extension and attached to upload fields with the browser's DataTransfer API. Some sites use custom upload widgets that refuse programmatic files; attach those manually.

Privacy

Everything stays in this browser. There are no accounts and no network requests. With the passphrase lock on, profiles are encrypted at rest and the decryption key lives only in session memory until the browser closes or the auto-lock timer runs out.
