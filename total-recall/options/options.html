Total Recall settings

Control what gets remembered, for how long, and how much space it may use.

Open search

Your memory stays on this device

Total Recall stores page text in this browser's local database (IndexedDB). It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and makes no network requests. Private (incognito) windows, browser pages, password and payment forms, banking sites and the sites you exclude below are never saved. Removing the extension deletes everything.

Indexing

Index pages as I browse
Turn off to pause. You can still index a page manually from the toolbar popup.
Skip sensitive pages
Pages with password or card fields, sign-in, checkout and banking pages.
Longer pages are truncated. Larger values find more, but use more space.
Skips near-empty pages such as redirects and app shells.

Excluded sites

Pages from these sites are never indexed. A domain also covers its subdomains. Add a path (example.com/private) to exclude only part of a site.

    Your data

    Export index
    Download every saved page and your settings as a JSON file, for backup or moving to another browser.
    Import index
    Merge pages from a Total Recall export. Existing pages are kept; newer copies win.
    Wipe all data
    Permanently delete every saved page from this device. Settings are kept.

    This deletes and cannot be undone. Consider exporting first.

    Shortcuts

    Type tr then Space in the address bar to search from anywhere. To open Total Recall with a key, assign one to “Open Total Recall search” at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
