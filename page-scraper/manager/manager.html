How it works

  1. Detect data finds tables and repeating blocks (product cards, search results, lists) and ranks them. Use the arrows in the panel to cycle through candidates; matches are outlined on the page.
  2. Point & click: hover and click one item. Similar items are selected using their shared class and structure path. Use Wider / Narrower (or ↑ / ↓ while picking) to adjust.
  3. Columns: click “Add column by clicking”, then click a field inside any outlined item. Choose Text, Link URL, Image URL, an attribute, or HTML. Rename, reorder and delete columns; the preview updates live.
  4. Pagination: pick the “next” or “load more” button once, or choose infinite scroll. Set the maximum number of pages and the delay between pages. Normal links are loaded in the background; JavaScript buttons are clicked on the page.
  5. Results open in a viewer where you can sort, filter, reorder and hide columns, then export CSV, Excel-compatible CSV, TSV or JSON, or copy straight into Google Sheets or Excel.

Keyboard

  • While picking: ↑ select parent, ↓ back to child, Enter confirm, Esc cancel.
  • Results table: Alt+←/→ on a focused header moves the column.
  • Optional shortcuts for “Detect data” and “Point & click” can be assigned at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Privacy

Everything stays in your browser’s extension storage. The extension only reads pages when you ask it to, and only fetches the next pages of the site you are scraping.
