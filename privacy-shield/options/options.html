Tracker blocking

Each category is a separate rule list. Trackers are only blocked when loaded by a different site (third-party), so a company's own site keeps working.

Links and signals

Removes utm_*, fbclid, gclid, mc_eid and over 100 other click identifiers from page addresses before they load.

Adds the Sec-GPC: 1 header and sets navigator.globalPrivacyControl, a legally recognised "do not sell or share" signal in several jurisdictions.

Cookie banners

Handles OneTrust, Cookiebot, Quantcast and other TCF banners, Didomi, TrustArc, Usercentrics, Sourcepoint, Osano, CookieYes, Klaro, Google Funding Choices and common hand-made banners. When a banner cannot be answered, it is hidden and page scrolling is restored.
