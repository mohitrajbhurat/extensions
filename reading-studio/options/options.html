    Keyboard shortcuts

    Opens chrome://extensions/shortcuts
    • Right-click a page or selection for Read selection aloud, Read this page aloud, Open in Reader view and Toggle dark mode.
    • Alt+click any paragraph on a page (after using Reading Studio there once) to start reading from it.
    • In reader view, Esc closes the overlay.

    Data and privacy

    Everything stays in your browser. Settings sync through your browser profile if sync is on; saved reading positions and site rules stay on this device. Reading Studio makes no network requests of its own.
